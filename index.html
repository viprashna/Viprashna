Our Philosophy

The Insight That Was
Always Within You

Viprashna does not seek to transform lives. Through insight, it seeks to resolve the dilemma within — naturally, at your own pace, through the accompaniment of someone who has seen enough of life to understand yours.

01

The Dilemma Persists

A dilemma persists because it has only ever been seen from familiar angles — your own. New eyes, new experience, and new questions open perspectives that were always there but never visible.

02

The Mentor Accompanies

A mentor through Viprashna does not arrive with answers. They arrive with experience, empathy, and the human skills to create the space in which your own insight can emerge.

03

The Insight Arrives

Through listening, reflection, shared experience, and questions that had not yet been asked — the insight arrives. Quietly. Naturally. As if it had always been waiting.

04

It Was Always Yours

The insight that follows is always the individual's own. It cannot be handed over. It can only be uncovered. That is the entire art of mentoring — and the entire purpose of Viprashna.

India has always known this. History records it with extraordinary clarity.
Chanakya & Chandragupta
He spotted a young boy playing a game of kings and saw in him the character of an emperor. Through years of patient mentoring, Chandragupta built the Mauryan Empire.
Ramakrishna & Vivekananda
A restless, doubting young Narendra found in Ramakrishna the mentor who helped him find clarity within himself. That young man stood before the world at Chicago in 1893.
Guru Ramdas & Shivaji
Samarth Guru Ramdas saw in a young Shivaji not merely a warrior but a king — and mentored him accordingly. The insight he brought shaped one of India's greatest leaders.

Viprashna is the revival of that tradition — in the classrooms, offices, homes, and crossroads of modern India.

How It Works

How a Mentor Brings Insight

There is no fixed script at Viprashna. The mentor's approach is calibrated to what you actually need at that moment.

01

Listening

Deeply, without agenda, without the urge to fix. For many individuals, this is the first time they have been truly heard. That listening alone begins to shift something.

02

Observing & Reflecting

The mentor observes not just what is said but what is left unsaid. From that observation comes a reflection that helps you see yourself from outside your own perspective.

03

Questions That Open New Angles

The mentor brings questions that have not yet been asked — arriving from outside your habitual thinking, opening perspectives that were always there but never visible.

04

Insight Arrives

In that new perspective, insight arrives — quietly, naturally. And with insight, the dilemma finds its resolution. The way forward becomes visible.

About Viprashna

Founded in Classrooms
Across India

Viprashna was founded and is led by Mrs. Shalini Sharma, wife of a senior retired officer of the Indian Army. Over three decades of moving with her husband's postings — from the north to the south, from cantonment towns to industrial cities — she taught in schools and coaching centres across the country.

It was in those classrooms that she began to see what most others missed. Behind every child preparing for a competitive examination is a dilemma that is entirely invisible to parents and teachers — not about syllabus, but about the invisible weight of self-doubt and expectation that no one had ever thought to address.

That realisation — and the conviction that every individual deserves the guidance of someone who truly sees them — became Viprashna. In this endeavour, Mrs. Sharma is supported by senior defence officers, educators, medical professionals, and legal advisors — all united by the same belief that mentoring can change the direction of a life.
